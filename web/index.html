What the backtest says

Forecast skill comes from wind plus measured PM2.5. Vision (SAM 3.1 + Muse) shows people where the smoke is; across three months it did not raise the score, and we report that.

Reference PM2.5 is CAMS (model, via Open-Meteo Air Quality), not ground sensors; a city counts as smoky when hourly PM2.5 reaches 55.5 µg/m³ (US EPA "Unhealthy"). Satellite image: NASA GIBS VIIRS true colour. Hotspots: NASA FIRMS VIIRS. Wind: ERA5 100 m. Forecast = 20 wind scenarios; chance = share of scenarios that bring smoke within 25 km of the city.

CSI = hits ÷ (hits + misses + false alarms); 1 is perfect. Built by M. Sopian Hadianto.
